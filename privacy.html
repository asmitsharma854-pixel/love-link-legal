Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Love Link Discord bot (“Love Link”, “the Bot”) handles information when people use it.

1. Information the Bot may process

Depending on the commands and features used, the Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames or display names, avatar/profile image URLs, command interactions, and pairing or compatibility selections/results.

2. Why information is processed

This information is used to respond to commands, display pairing results, operate Bot features, and help prevent misuse.

3. Storage and retention

The Bot is hosted by Wispbyte. The Bot operator says the Bot does not permanently store its pairing/user data and that some runtime data may be temporary and may be cleared when the Bot restarts. This statement does not cover separate records that Discord or the hosting provider may create, such as platform or infrastructure logs; those providers control their own retention practices.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by Discord and the hosting provider as needed to operate the Bot. Their own privacy policies apply to information they process.

5. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect the Bot and information it processes. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and questions

You can stop using the Bot at any time. For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by the Bot operator, contact us through the Discord support server.

7. Children and age requirements

Use of Discord and the Bot is subject to Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable law. The Bot is not intended to override those requirements.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be published here with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, join our Discord support server.
