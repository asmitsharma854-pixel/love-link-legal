# Love Link – Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** November 2, 2026 Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Love Link handles information when you use our Discord Bot. ### 1. Information We Process Depending on the features used, Love Link may process: - Discord User IDs - Discord Server IDs - Usernames and display names - Profile pictures and avatar URLs - Pairing and compatibility-related information - Interaction and command-related information ### 2. How We Use Information Information is processed to: - Provide pairing and shipping features. - Generate compatibility percentages. - Display user profiles and pairing results. - Maintain bot functionality and improve user experience. - Prevent abuse and misuse. ### 3. Data Storage and Retention Love Link is hosted using Wispbyte hosting services. The bot is not designed to permanently retain user pairing information. Some information may be temporarily processed or held in memory while the bot is running. Temporary information may be lost when the bot restarts. However, hosting logs or other technical records may be retained separately by service providers under their own policies. ### 4. Data Sharing We do not intentionally sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and hosting infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the bot. ### 5. Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Love Link. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. ### 6. Data Deletion Since pairing-related information is not designed to be permanently stored, temporary information may disappear when the bot restarts or its temporary session ends. For privacy-related questions or deletion requests, contact our support team. ### 7. Children's Privacy Love Link is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Discord's age requirements. ### 8. Third-Party Services Love Link operates through Discord and Wispbyte hosting infrastructure. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply. ### 9. Policy Updates We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be published on this page. ### 10. Contact For privacy questions, concerns, or requests, contact the Love Link support team through our official Discord support server.
